out there

Activities

01

Game Drive

Morning and afternoon drives across the Seronera plains in search of lion, leopard, elephant and the migration herds.

02

Bird Watching

Over 500 bird species live in the Serengeti, from kingfishers by the river to raptors over the grassland.

03

Nature Walk

Guided walks with an armed ranger to read tracks, learn the plants and feel the bush up close.

04

Bushmen tour

Spend time with Hadzabe hunter-gatherers and learn how they have lived off this land for centuries.

05

Cultural Dances

Evenings of traditional song and dance by local performers around the camp fire.

06

Acrobatic Shows

Energetic Tanzanian acrobats bring a lively show to camp after a long day on safari.

07

Video shows

Wildlife documentaries and migration films screened in camp after dinner.

08

Gift shop

Local crafts, beadwork and souvenirs to take a piece of the Serengeti home.
