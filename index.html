Serengeti Wildebeest Camps

Located in the central part of Serengeti National Park, in the Seronera Tana-Extra area, the camp has 10 spacious, self-contained private tents, each with its own private shower and toilet. Watch the Great Migration thunder past, enjoy warm hospitality and some of the best birdwatching in Tanzania, with over 500 species recorded nearby.
