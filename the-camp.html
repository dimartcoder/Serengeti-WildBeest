camp overview

Ten tents. One endless horizon.

Watch the Great Migration thunder past at Serengeti Wildebeest Camps, set in the heart of Central Serengeti.

Serengeti Wildebeest Camps is located in the central part of Serengeti National Park, in the Seronera Tana-Extra area. The camp has 10 spacious, self-contained private tents, each with its own private shower and toilet. This gives guests a comfortable and unforgettable, first-hand experience of the wildlife, landscapes, and natural wonders of one of Tanzania’s most famous national parks.

Serengeti Wildebeest Camps sits in the buffer zone of the Serengeti ecosystem, giving guests authentic access to the wilderness with a strong focus on biodiversity.
