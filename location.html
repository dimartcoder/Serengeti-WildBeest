where we are

Location & Surroundings

Around Serengeti Wildebeest Camps, open grassland stretches to the horizon, broken up by patches of riverine forest along the nearby Seronera River. This forest is a haven for wildlife, sheltering leopards, vervet monkeys, and olive baboons, along with many colorful birds. Because the camp lies directly in the path of the Great Migration, guests staying here have a strong chance of watching millions of wildebeest and zebra move across the plains in search of fresh grass.
